# Warm toy laboratory

Approved direction: cream, muted sage and copper; soft daylight; matte rounded toy blocks. Existing MAGNET LAB name, gift rules and controls stay.

Alternatives considered: warm toy laboratory (selected) and graphite night laboratory (not selected). The warm version lets viewer photos carry most of the color.

Plan: (1) six-face portrait renderer sharing one EditableImage; (2) one reusable baked rounded UnionOperation, with inset rounded face prints; (3) analytic gravity/bounce/damped spin and magnetic capture curves on moving blocks only, server-authoritative scores unchanged; (4) warm environment materials/lighting and quiet HUD; (5) Studio checks for all faces, smooth motion boundaries and 1600-block load, visual QA and existing Node suite.

Physics is visual simulation, not a networked pile of colliding rigid bodies. This retains predictable capture positions and limits per-frame work. Back up all changed Studio geometry and Lighting before applying. Do not publish the place automatically.
